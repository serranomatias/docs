A webhook sends call data to your system through a **POST** request. A call can finish successfully even if delivery to your endpoint fails.

## Configure and test

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare your receiver">Create a reachable URL that accepts POST JSON. If you start calls through the API, store their IDs to correlate them with your system.</Step>
  <Step title="Publish the agent URL">Open **Voice agent → Advanced → Post-call**, configure the outgoing webhook, and publish. The inbound webhook retrieves context and has a different contract.</Step>
  <Step title="Make a controlled call">Use **Test agent** and wait for analysis. If evaluation criteria are assigned, delivery can wait for their results.</Step>
  <Step title="Compare delivery and action">Inspect the body in your receiver and **Calls → Webhooks**. Also confirm that the expected task, ticket, or update completed.</Step>
</Steps>

The current editor has no sample-payload button. Test calls can execute real actions in your receiver.

## What you receive

The body has a `data` object containing agent, organization, call, and available context. Contact and campaign blocks depend on the record. **A web test can have a contact when it was registered with email.**

## Investigate delivery

Open an event in **Calls → Webhooks** to inspect its URL, status, attempts, HTTP response, raw data, and associated call.

| Status | Next step |
|---|---|
| Delivered | Confirm that your application processed the content as well as acknowledging delivery. |
| Pending | Check again before starting manual recovery. |
| Failed | Read the response code and message, fix the receiver, and select **Retry** when available. |

Store `data.call.call_id` in your receiver. The event ID shown in the app helps diagnostics but is not automatically included in the body. Make your receiver tolerate repeated deliveries: a retry must not create duplicate tickets or repeat an operation.

These deliveries are separate from messaging channel webhooks, inbox automations, and lead-routing events. Each has its own configuration and content.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Does retrying place another call?">
    No. It retries event delivery. Your receiver may repeat actions unless it handles duplicates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the inbound webhook the same?">
    No. The inbound webhook obtains context before the conversation; the outgoing webhook delivers call information.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What should I send support?">
    The event ID, call ID, date, and HTTP response, with receiver secrets removed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
